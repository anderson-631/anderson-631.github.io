Tapeout 1 Board

SiliconJackets · Spring 2026

What would you do if an electrician wired your porch light and bedroom lamp to the same switch? Probably ask for a refund at least, but we at SiliconJackets did not have that luxury when our first Tapeout was returned to us with a similar problem: half of its pins were floating and each pin in the other half simultaneously drove an input and output.

It turns out we had overlapping I/O assignments that were not caught before the design files were sent to the foundry. I attempted to square this circle by designing a special evaluation board that would work around the overlapping I/O by multiplexing each active pin with an FPGA in addition to providing programming support for the chip with a serial interface and Raspberry Pi.
